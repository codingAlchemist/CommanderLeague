02

League rules

  1. Bring a pre-con. Play with an unaltered, commercially released pre-con deck.
  2. Play your scheduled pod. Each week, the organizer will place registered players into pods for multiplayer games. At the end of each week, each player will be able to upgrade their deck by adding cards from a purchased play booster pack or a single card that is currently valued at 20 dollars or less.
  3. Keep games welcoming. Follow Commander's social contract: communicate clearly, play fairly, and help new players get involved.
  4. Complete the league. Show up for weekly games and track results with the organizer so the league stays current.
Not sure about a ruling?

Ask the organizer before the game begins. The goal is a friendly, competitive night for everyone.
